About Me

I am a Quality Engineer dedicated to ensuring software reliability through methodical analysis and robust automation. My journey into QA stems from a deep curiosity about how complex systems fail and a drive to prevent those failures before they reach production. My core philosophy centers on a 'shift-left' approach—collaborating early in the development lifecycle to catch design and logic flaws before they become costly bugs. Whether I am writing clean, scalable automated test frameworks for UIs and APIs, or meticulously digging into the code to uncover tricky edge cases, my goal is always to deliver software that is both bulletproof and seamless for the end user.
